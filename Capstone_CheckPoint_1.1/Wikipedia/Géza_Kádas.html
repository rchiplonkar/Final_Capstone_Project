Géza Kádas (7 August 1926 – 6 March 1979) was a Hungarian swimmer and Olympic medalist. He participated at the 1948 and 1952 Summer Olympics, winning a silver medal in 4 × 200 metre freestyle relay, and a bronze medal in 100 metre freestyle in 1948.[1] Kádas died in Budapest on 6 March 1979, at the age of 52.[2]
