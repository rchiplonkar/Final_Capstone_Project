Donahoe is a ghost town in Bell County, in the U.S. state of Texas.[1] It is located within the Killeen-Temple-Fort Hood metropolitan area.

Geography

[edit]

Donahoe was located on Donahoe Creek, 16 mi (26 km) southeast of Belton in the southeastern corner of Bell County.[2]

Education

[edit]

Today, Donahoe is located within the Bartlett Independent School District.
