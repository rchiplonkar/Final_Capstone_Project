Japanese film director and cinematographer
Daisaku Kimura
Daisaku Kimura
Born (1939-07-13) July 13, 1939 (age 86)
Tokyo-Fu, Japan
OccupationsFilm director, cinematographer

Daisaku Kimura (木村 大作, Kimura Daisaku) is a Japanese film director and cinematographer.

Overviews

[edit]

He won the award for best director at the 33rd Japan Academy Prize for Mt. Tsurugidake.[1]

Filmography

[edit]
  • Submersion of Japan (1973, cinematographer)
  • Mt. Tsurugidake (2009, director)
  • Climbing to Spring (2014, director)
  • Samurai's Promise (2018, director)
  • Hara o Kukutte (2027, director)[2]

Honours

[edit]
  • Person of Cultural Merit (2020)
